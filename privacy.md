# TabItBro – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

TabItBro does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension does with data

- **No collection, no transmission.** TabItBro sends nothing to its developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code, and makes no network requests of its own.
- **Your options** (which navigations open in a new tab, and so on) are saved with Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If you use browser sync, your browser syncs them to your own account, just like your other browser settings. The developer has no access to them.
- **Tab URLs** are read on your device only to decide whether a navigation should move to a new tab, for example whether the tab was on the History page or an empty New Tab page. The extension keeps each tab's previous URL in session storage (`chrome.storage.session`), which stays in memory on your device and is cleared when you close the tab or the browser. These URLs are never logged, saved permanently or sent anywhere.
- **Light/dark theme:** a single on/off value is stored locally so that the toolbar icon matches your theme.
- **Web pages:** the content script only checks which link you clicked, to see whether it leads to another domain. It does not read, change or store page content.

## Third-party links

The options page and the README include a "Buy me a coffee" button. Its image is part of the extension, so nothing is loaded from buymeacoffee.com unless you click the button, which opens that site in a new tab. That site has its own privacy policy.
